❌ Slow Follow-Up Kills Deals
Lead fills out your form at 9pm. You call back at 10am the next day. They've already booked someone else. Leads contacted within 5 minutes are 21x more likely to convert. Most companies take hours.

❌ One-Time Jobs Stay One-Time
Customer loves your work. But 6 months later, they can't remember your name. They Google and find someone else. Without a retention system, 80%+ of your customers never come back.

Q: How long does it take to set up?
A: 14 days from kickoff. You keep running your business while we build everything. Most clients see results within the first week once it's live.
Q: Do you run our ads?
A: This system focuses on what happens AFTER the lead comes in. We can help with ad creative and positioning, and offer ad management as a separate service if needed.
Q: What if I'm not running ads yet?
A: If you're planning to start soon, we can build the backend first. In fact, that's the smart way — have the system ready before you turn on ad spend.
Q: What's the investment?
A: Depends on your situation. Setup typically ranges from $2,500-$4,500 with monthly support. We'll give you exact pricing on the strategy call.
Q: What if it doesn't work?
A: We track everything — response times, conversion rates, retention metrics. You'll see the numbers every week. If something isn't working, we fix it.
Q: What tech/software do I need?
A: We build on GoHighLevel and integrate with your existing systems. ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro — we work with all of them. No need to switch anything.
